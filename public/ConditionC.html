Details about the design challenge
  1. Your team's goal is to brainstorm together for 35 minutes for the design challenge mentioned below.
  2. Finalize any idea(s) that you are satisfied with. There is no required number of ideas.
  3. The ideas need to satisfy the constraints of the design challenge.
  4. Your goal is NOT to complete the task as quickly as possible.

Design challenge:

Setup: You are a designer at a technology company. Your task is to brainstorm ideas for a smartphone feature to reduce the amount of time users spend “compulsive scrolling”. Compulsive scrolling refers to obsessive, endless scrolling, often performed on social media feeds.

If you don't know what compulsive scrolling is, please let the researcher know.

Constraints: Any feature proposed by you should not block the user from using an app and should not delete any app or turn off the phone.
